Frequently asked questions

Is this a regulated mortgage quote?

No. It illustrates repayment on a fixed-rate amortising loan. It is not a lender offer, affordability assessment or regulated APR calculation.

Can it be adapted for other countries?

Currencies and assumptions can be adapted. Local finance rules, taxes and disclosures must be checked before client deployment.

Is it subscription-based?

The pilot is a one-off setup service. Ongoing maintenance would be offered only under a separate agreement.

Do you collect visitors’ financial details?

The demo runs calculations locally in the browser and does not transmit the amounts entered to an MDE server.
